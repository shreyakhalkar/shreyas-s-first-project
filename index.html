Introduction

The movie Chhaava is a historical action film released in 2025. It is directed by Laxman Utekar and stars Vicky Kaushal as Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj. The film is based on the life of Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj, the brave son of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. It shows his courage, leadership, and dedication to Swarajya. Rashmika Mandanna plays the role of Maharani Yesubai. The movie highlights the bravery and sacrifices of the Maratha Empire. Chhaava inspires audiences with its powerful story of courage, patriotism, and determination.
